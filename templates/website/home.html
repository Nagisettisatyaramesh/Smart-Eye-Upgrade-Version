Product Modules

Everything a device quality team touches, in one place

Each module reflects a stage of the product quality lifecycle, from initial design input through post-market surveillance.

DC

Document Control

Version-controlled SOPs, work instructions, and specifications with review and approval routing.

DH

Design Controls

Design history file records linking user needs, design inputs, outputs, and verification & validation.

RM

Risk Management

Hazard analysis, risk estimation, and mitigation tracking aligned to ISO 14971.

CA

CAPA Management

Root cause investigation, corrective action plans, and effectiveness checks in one record.

CH

Complaint Handling

Intake, triage, and regulatory reportability assessment for post-market complaints.

AM

Audit Management

Internal and supplier audit scheduling, findings, and closure tracking.
